NFL Last Man Standing

New player?
Start here.

Everything you need to enter in under two minutes.

SCAN TO JOIN
OPEN nfl-lms.vercel.app
1

Register

Tap REGISTER.

Enter your first name, last name, email and a password of at least 7 characters.

You’re in immediately—there is no confirmation email.

2

Add your entry

Sign in and open DASHBOARD.

Tap ADD AN ENTRY to join the current round.

Playing more than once? Add another. Each entry is numbered and managed separately.

3

Get marked paid

Contact Connor or Havo to pay.

An admin must mark each entry Paid before it can make a pick.

Check the status badge on your Dashboard.

4

Make your pick

Open PICKS and choose the correct entry.

Select one NFL team, review it, then tap Confirm pick.

Your team badge will appear in your pick history.

1H

Need to change your pick?

Change it in PICKS until the weekly deadline: one hour before the first kick-off. Check the exact UK time on Dashboard.

The essentials

One team each week • Win = stay in • Loss, tie or no pick = out
A team can be used once until a rollover or new round.
