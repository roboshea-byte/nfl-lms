# NFL LMS accounts

Extend the existing NFL LMS app with immediate email/password signup, owner/admin/member roles, member dashboards and manual payment management. Owner email: roboshea@gmail.com. Members may edit their own paid entries until one hour before the first game of each week. Public dashboards show that deadline in UK time. Rollover resets team availability and requires a fresh manual payment.

Required: accounts, permissions, admin member management, pick deadlines, responsive layouts, audit history. Deferred: online payments and automated email. Existing results, imports, exports, multi-entry and rollover tools remain available to administrators.
